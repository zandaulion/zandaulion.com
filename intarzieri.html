Intârzieri Tren

About The App

Intârzieri Tren is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to track train delays across Romania. Built for convenience, it allows you to effortlessly monitor your journey without needing to install a heavy native app.

Simply add it to your home screen on Android or iOS for a full-screen, native-like experience. Access is invite-only, ensuring a reliable and streamlined service without the need for usernames or passwords.

Features

  • Train Tracking: Track up to 5 trains simultaneously across the Romanian railway network, using real-time data sourced from cfr.ro.
  • Route Visualization: View the complete route of your train along with its measured delay at each station.
  • Push Notifications: Get instantly notified when your tracked train's delay changes, so you never miss an update.
  • Installable PWA: Add to your home screen on iOS and Android for quick access.
