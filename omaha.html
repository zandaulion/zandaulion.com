About The App

A Mobile-First Progressive Web App (PWA) for Stock Health, Economic Moats, and Fundamental Rigor.

Built for modern value investors who demand deep balance-sheet clarity, durable free cash flows, and zero noise. Pocket Omaha brings rigorous financial analysis to your pocket, offline-first and beautifully designed.

Features

  • 🛡️ Composite Health Scorecard (0–100): Evaluates companies across 5 pillars including Financial Solvency, Profitability, Moat Quality, Valuation, and Capital Allocation.
  • 🚦 12-Point Traffic-Light Checklist: Instant PASS / WATCH / FAIL assessment against rigorous value-investing benchmarks.
  • 📈 5-Year Historical Financial Trajectory: Visualize Revenue vs. Free Cash Flow, margin expansion trends, and balance sheet cushion directly against debt.
  • 🎯 Interactive DCF Sandbox: 2-stage Discounted Cash Flow model with real-time sliders for growth rates, exit multiples, and discount hurdle rates.
  • 📔 Personal Investment Journaling: Write buy theses, track target entry prices, set exit guardrails, and log dated entries.
  • 🔍 Fundamental Screener: Multi-factor filtering with instant preset queries and side-by-side comparison for up to 4 peer tickers.
  • ⚡ Zero-Recurring-Cost Architecture: Uses yahoo-finance2 for zero-cost ingestion, SQLite for local caching, and functions fully offline as a PWA on iOS and Android.
