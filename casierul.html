Casierul clasei

Keep track of a school class's money: who has paid, what is still owed, and where every leu went.

Launch Web App View on GitHub

The live app is invitation-only and in Romanian.

About The App

Casierul clasei (“the class treasurer”) is for the parent who looks after a school class's fund. It keeps each child's contributions, the class's expenses, and every payment in one shared record, so the treasurer always knows the balance and who still owes what. One installation can look after several classes and schools, each with its own separate ledger.

The app is in Romanian, with amounts in lei. Access is by invitation, for one class and one role at a time. The treasurer can see and change everything. A parent can see the class totals and their own child's account. An auditor can see the whole ledger and the reports without changing anything. Read-only access can expire on a chosen date.

How it works

  1. Set up the class. Add the school, the class, and the school year, then the children, one at a time or by pasting a list.
  2. Create expenses. Choose the children taking part and how the cost is shared: a fixed amount per child, a total split between them, or a quantity per child times a unit price.
  3. Collect money. Tap a child and pick everything they owe or a single expense. Big buttons round the amount up to the next 10, 50, or 100 lei, and any difference is given back as change or kept as an advance.
  4. Pay and keep records. Record money paid out, with where it went and, optionally, the expense it belongs to, and follow the class balance in the ledger.

Features

  • Every child's account: Contributions, amounts still owed, advances, and the full history for each child.
  • Advances that add up: An advance can later cover another contribution or be refunded, and using it never counts as new money coming in.
  • Corrections without erasing: A confirmed operation is undone by a separate correction with a reason, so the history always stays complete.
  • Exact to the last ban: Amounts are stored in whole bani, the Romanian cents, and splitting a total shares out every last one exactly.
  • Safe on a shaky connection: Saving needs the internet, but if a reply gets lost, a retry confirms the same request without recording the payment twice. Changes made on another device have to be checked before saving.
  • PDF reports: Preview, share, or download reports right in the app, colour-coded green for paid, yellow for partly paid, red for overdue, and blue for balances and money paid out. Each PDF keeps the school and class logos and colours it was issued with.
  • Several classes, kept apart: Each class has its own database, children, expenses, reports, logos, and permissions. A selector in the header switches between classes, and the same device can have different roles in different classes.
  • Parents see only their own child: A parent's device receives the class totals and their child's details, and never the names or payments of the other children.
  • JSON export: A readable copy of the data and its history, without any credentials.

Under the hood

Casierul clasei runs on Node.js 24 with its built-in SQLite and no runtime dependencies. It updates itself with pwa-kit, and its invitations and devices are managed from PWA Invite Console. Daily backups are checked for integrity before they are kept. The project is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.
