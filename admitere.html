Admitere Liceu Kit

Explore admission history and simulate high school choices in Bucharest.

View on GitHub

About The Project

A complete methodology, historical data, analysis scripts, and AI prompts for high school admissions and the National Evaluation exam in Bucharest.

This package contains a complete method for organizing high school admission choices, structured so that any family can use it with an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).

Gallery

Features

  • 📊 Three Years of Historical Data: More than 400 study tracks with admission averages (2024–2026), special places for students with special educational needs (CES), and baccalaureate pass rates (2018–2026).
  • 📈 Ranking Curves & Calibration: Grade-to-rank interpolation algorithms help account for annual changes in exam difficulty.
  • 🎲 Computerized Placement Simulator: A Python script simulates the official ISMB placement algorithm using the student’s ordered list of choices.
  • 🤖 Detailed AI Assistant Playbook: Structured prompts help a language model act as a dedicated admissions assistant.
  • 🏫 Preparing for the 2027 Reform: A guide to the new admission exams organized directly by high schools for up to 50% of their places.
  • 🔓 Open Source & Free License: Free and open source under the MIT license, available to every parent and teacher.
