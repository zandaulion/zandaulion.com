# Zandaulion lion

Approved direction: a calm, curious adult lion in loose pen strokes. Preserve this
expression and silhouette; do not simplify it into a cartoon mascot.

- `lion-light-v2.png`: forest-green mark on transparency, for light backgrounds.
- `lion-dark-v2.png`: cream mark on forest green, for dark backgrounds.
- The original `logo.jpg` and `logo_light.jpg` remain available for older references.
- Website lettering is rendered separately so the mark stays legible in the header.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the approved concept board.

## Final asset prompts

Light: “Precise asset extraction from the approved logo board, NOT a redesign.
Extract ONLY the lion head from the LEFT cream panel. Preserve its exact adult
face, expression, eyes, proportions, three-quarter pose looking right, loose
hand-inked mane and line details. Remove all lettering and the right panel.
Output one square PNG with the single forest-green #223f35 lion mark centered,
filling about 88 percent of the canvas height with enough margin to retain every
whisker and mane tip. Make the background genuinely transparent with alpha; all
gaps within the ink drawing must also be transparent. Do not add a background,
texture, glow, new lines, shadow, or text. This is the approved mark prepared as
a clean standalone website logo for light backgrounds.”

Dark: “Extract the exact approved lion mark from the RIGHT dark-green panel of
this reference board into one square standalone logo image. Remove all lettering.
Preserve the same mature face, three-quarter pose, relaxed curious expression,
all mane curves, proportions, whiskers, and hand-inked character. Do not redesign.
Clean solid warm-cream #f7f5ee ink on a uniform fully OPAQUE dark forest green
#223f35 background across the entire square. Center the head, filling 88% of canvas
height, with enough margin for every mane tip. No transparency. No glow, grain,
stippling, speckles, texture, gradients, halos, or extra marks. The line interiors
must be solid flat cream; the negative spaces solid flat green. Crisp clean edges.
Just the approved lion head and plain background, no text or framing.”
