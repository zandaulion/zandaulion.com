# Bitey screenshots

The Android welcome and tablet images added on 27 October 2026 come from the
app's store screenshot set. The welcome was captured on 25 October; the tablet
day view was captured on 11 October. The Wear OS images were captured on
27 October for the companion prepared for phone release 1.1.9.

All added images use the app's synthetic screenshot fixture, not a personal
diary. The watch uses the same fictional food and weight history as the phone.
No account, device identifiers, credentials or personal photographs are included.
The PNGs contain no embedded metadata. Their original pixels and dimensions are
preserved. The website supplies translated descriptions; screenshot UI is English.

Source files are maintained in the Bitey repository under `store/screenshots/`.
