Magpie

A place for stray thoughts, photos, and voice notes before they fly away.

About The Project

Throw scraps in. It works out what they add up to.

Text, pictures, and voice. The point is that recording a thought costs nothing — no title, no category, no tags — and that everything Magpie makes of them afterwards sits on top rather than replacing them.

The interface and Magpie's replies are in Romanian. The people using it think in Romanian, and reading a second language is friction — which is the one thing this app exists to remove.

Features

  • ⚡ Zero-Friction Capture: Capture fleeting thoughts in seconds with zero mandatory structure, folders, or tagging overhead.
  • 🎙️ Voice & Visual Input: Speak your mind with native audio transcription or snap visual scraps to store memories instantly.
  • 💬 AI Resonance: Magpie says one thing back to each scrap — a dry remark or an open question chosen at random to spur further reflection.
  • 💥 Knock Two Together: Randomly collides two scraps from your heap to uncover unexpected connections and creative synthesis.
  • 🇷🇴 Native Romanian Language Experience: Engineered specifically for Romanian native speakers to think, speak, and read without mental friction.
  • 🔒 Private & Device-Paired: Lightweight, invite-secured PWA keeping all personal thoughts strictly confidential.
