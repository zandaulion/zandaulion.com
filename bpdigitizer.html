About The App

Your blood pressure, part of your fitness routine.

Keep your blood pressure in view alongside the rest of your active life. This app makes it effortless to log your numbers, watch your trends over time, and see how your workouts, rest, and daily habits line up with them — in seconds a day, free, with no account.

Features

  • 🏃 Built for an active lifestyle: Blood pressure is a core wellness metric, right alongside your steps, workouts, and sleep. Log it in seconds and keep it part of the routine that keeps you moving.
  • ✏️ Log your readings for free, in seconds: Fast, tap-friendly sliders let you set systolic, diastolic, and pulse in moments. Completely free, works offline, and needs no account — start tracking the very first time you open the app.
  • 📷 Or scan with the camera (Premium): Don't want to type? Snap a photo of your monitor's display and the numbers are entered for you. Camera scanning is an optional Premium feature for the easiest possible entry.
  • 📈 Watch your trends: See how your numbers move over days, weeks, and months. Switch between a trend line and a 3D scatter you can spin with a finger to explore systolic, diastolic, and pulse together. Smart "Avg" smoothing merges back-to-back readings into a single, honest data point, so your trend reflects reality.
  • 🏷️ See what moves your numbers: Tag any reading with context — exercise, caffeine, a salty meal, poor sleep, stress — and discover how your activity and lifestyle shape your numbers over time.
  • ❤️ Connects with Health Connect: Sync with Android Health Connect to keep your blood pressure right alongside your activity, workouts, and other fitness data — on your terms, with your permission.
  • 📄 Keep your own records: Export a simple PDF of your history — charts and readings included.
  • ⏰ Build a consistent habit: Optional gentle reminders help you log at the same time each morning and evening, so your trends stay complete.
  • 🔒 Private by design: Your data stays on your device. No account to create, no cloud sign-up, nothing sold or shared. Your numbers are your business — and only yours.
