About The App

Log, chart and export blood pressure readings. Local-first: readings live in your browser's IndexedDB and are never sent anywhere.

A web companion to the Android app, sharing its clinical logic and its translations. Open the page, add readings, see charts, export a file. That is the whole app. There is no build step, no framework, and no backend requirement.

Features

  • 📱 Local-first & PWA: Installs to the home screen and works offline. Readings live in your browser's IndexedDB and are never sent anywhere.
  • ✏️ Fast entry: Manual entry with sliders seeded from your last reading.
  • 📈 Comprehensive Charts: Trend chart (systolic + diastolic, 120/80 reference lines) and a systolic-vs-diastolic scatter. 7 / 30 / 90 day and all-time ranges.
  • 🧠 Smart Analytics: Burst averaging collapses readings taken back to back into a single point. Insights computed on the device (morning vs evening, time in range, healthy streaks, context tags).
  • 🩺 Clinical Logic: AHA zones (Normal / Elevated / Stage 1 / Stage 2 / Crisis), MAP and pulse pressure. Risk assessment from your health profile.
  • 📄 Export & Reports: CSV and JSON export. Detailed PDF reports containing overview pages, charts, and table history.
  • 🌐 Global: 12 languages, carried over from the Android app rather than re-translated.
